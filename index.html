Our mission

We aren’t just building a Bike Park.

We’re creating a Mountain Bike Oasis, that lets you thrive from the moment you arrive! Our mission is to provide the most enjoyable riding experience possible.

The ride

Tow-Rope

Tow-Ropes are a proven concept in the snowboard industry, but under-utilized in Commercial Bike Park applications. This zero-wait solution maximizes your time riding downhill, making laps highly repeatable and skill improvement second nature. Having a Rope Tow sets us apart from the competition, and in a lane of our own.

After dark

Ride the Park after Dark! Stadium Lighting keeps the cash coming in day and night! And what goes well with a night sesh? A camping spot to seal the deal..

        For the Investor

        They make a clear profit. Instead of just getting their $200,000 back over time, they walk away with $326,000 total (a $126,000 net profit / ~63% total ROI), fully paid back by Year 6. They also get a full year to let the business open and settle in.

        For The Founder

        They get a 1-year "breathing room" period to grow the business utilizing cash without immediate debt pressure. As they pay investment out, they slowly buy back 100% control of the company out of its own profits, leaving owners completely debt-free and equity-free by Year 6.
