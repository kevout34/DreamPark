Our mission

We aren’t just building a Bike Park.

We’re creating a Mountain Bike Oasis, that lets you thrive from the moment you arrive! Our mission is to provide the most enjoyable riding experience possible.

The ride

Tow-Rope

Tow-Ropes are a proven concept in the snowboard industry, but under-utilized in Commercial Bike Park applications. This zero-wait solution maximizes your time riding downhill, making laps highly repeatable and skill improvement second nature. Having a Rope Tow sets us apart from the competition, and in a lane of our own.

The vision

The best-built trails around

We’re building hand-shaped, flowing trails with a clear path of progression for every rider. Beginners get smooth, forgiving lines and a skills area to build confidence. Experts get bigger jumps, steeper terrain and technical features to chase. It’s a park the whole family can enjoy together, whatever level each person rides.

  • 01 LearnFlow trails and a skills area
  • 02 ProgressBigger berms, rollers and jumps
  • 03 Send itExpert lines and race tracks
Then we bring it all together

Youth and adult Freeride, Big Air, and Race Events

Our events and competitions give the community a reason to come together: riders to compete, families to cheer them on, and neighbors to celebrate the sport. With live music playing right beside the trails, you can amplify your riding, and keep the stoke alive even after the bikes are put away!

After dark

Lights

Ride the Park after Dark! Stadium Lighting keeps the cash coming in day and night! And what goes well with a night sesh? A camping spot to seal the deal..

The stay

Camping

What’s better than shredding the park till closing? Spending the night, then shredding again the next day! We offer primitive camp sites right at the park, so you can ride until dark and wake up next to the trails.

Snack Bar, Dog Park & Walking Trails

We offer a snack bar with healthy food and drinks to keep our riders fueled up all day. With plenty of family friendly amenities, there's no reason your dog, kid, or significant other needs to be left behind.

      They make a clear profit. Instead of just getting their $225,000 back over time, they walk away with $367,000 total (a $142,000 net profit / ~63% total ROI), fully paid back by Year 6. They also get a full year to let the business open and settle in.
